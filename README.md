One word for the whole internet, funniest and most fitting wins. The entry is that word and nothing else.

**Entry: `brainrot`**

The internet's native condition — its content, its effect, and its culture in one word. Funny on its own, true of the internet as a whole, surprising, and it lands in a single beat.

`main.ts` writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` (8 characters, no trailing newline).
